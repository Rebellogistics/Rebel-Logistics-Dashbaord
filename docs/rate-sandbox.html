The same rate book across five typical jobs

Job Base Fuel levy Ex GST GST Inc GST vs engine today
  • Fuel levy — transport only, ex GSTA quote captures the rate book's setting at the moment it is raised and keeps it — March quotes stay levied when September's setting is off, approved or not. Add or remove it by hand on a quote raised in one month but carried out in another. When on it rides delivery and hourly work, and stays exempt on labour and warehousing, where no vehicle moves. Inbox charges 12% fixed on interstate, discretionary elsewhere.
  • Warehouse labour — no minimumsOutbound warehouse charges, quality control checks and extra container time each carry their own editable rate, billed per labourer in 30-minute increments and named on their own invoice line. Unlike a Labour job there is no crew or hours floor — both are editable in the rate book if that changes.
  • Warehousing — three tiers, grace then round upLong term pays the tier rate — Standard $25, High end $40, Insurance added $50 per m³ per month — and a short-term hold adds an uplift on top. All share the 5-day grace. Pick-up and delivery is an add-on, hourly with no minimum; Container unload sits alongside as its own service, flat at $550 for a 20 ft and $800 for a 40 ft, covering 2 hours on site with extra time ticked on as additional labour. Rebel prices storage entirely by hand today.
  • Rubbish disposal — preset loadsNever a job of its own — always a tick on the job that created the rubbish: an hourly job, a storage job, a container unload, an hourly job out of a container, or a White Glove job over 10 m³. Van $190 and trailer $290 each plus a $150 transport fee; a larger load is measured at the end of the job and carries $220. No fuel levy — transport is already its own line. Every rate behind these pull-downs lives in the Additional services group of the rate book.
  • Labour — per head, per hour$60 per labourer per hour × crew × hours. Floors at 2 pax and 3 h. The site already sells onsite labour hire; the engine has no job type for it.
  • Packaging is counted, not estimatedHourly and Labour jobs can each carry a packaging-materials line, filled in once the job is done and it is known what was supplied. No fuel levy — it is goods, not a run.
  • White Glove covers its own rubbishRemoval is priced into the White Glove rate up to 10 m³. Past that the volume stops being incidental and disposal becomes a chargeable extra, the same two lines as everywhere else.
  • Zone comes from the postcodeApplies to every delivery, including jobs out of a container. The postcode decides it outright — on Rebel’s list is Metro, everything else is Regional. Same 192-postcode set the dashboard uses in src/lib/metroPostcodes.ts.
  • Hourly — two trucks, 30-minute incrementsStandard $180/h, Large $200/h, both off the same 3 h minimum. Time rounds up to the next half hour — the same rule on labour and on storage legs.
